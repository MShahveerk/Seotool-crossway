Marketplace

One marketplace for cargo, charter and sale

Post what you have or what you need. ShipSearch returns the other side.

Match cargo to ships in one search

Post cargo — commodity, quantity, ports, laycan — and see vessels by type, DWT, position and open dates. List an open position to see cargoes it can carry: fewer dead-end enquiries, shorter ballast, faster fixtures.

Charter vessels across every segment

Search live tonnage from tweendeckers and MPPs to RO-RO, tankers, bulkers and inland barges — voyage or time charter. Every listing links to a verified company profile; you negotiate and fix in the built-in messenger.

Buy and sell ships on an open exchange

List a vessel with full particulars, or search by type, DWT, year and class, then deal with the seller or their broker. The whole S&P market, not the fraction in your inbox.

Desk

Freight calculators and voyage tools, built in

Chartering-desk tools on the marketplace — no spreadsheets, no extra subscriptions.

Voyage estimator
Distance, time, bunkers and port costs before you commit to a fixture.
TCE calculator
Turn any freight offer into time charter equivalent per day, so fixtures compare on equal terms.
Demurrage calculator
Laytime and demurrage from your CP terms — claims settle on numbers, not arguments.
Scenario analysis
Run routes, speeds and bunker prices side by side and fix the voyage that earns the most.
Port distance calculator
Port-to-port distances and routing in seconds, feeding straight into the estimate.
Cargo order management
Every enquiry, offer and fixture in one workflow instead of email threads.
Vessel position tracking
Live AIS for your fleet and counterparties, with arrival and departure detection.
